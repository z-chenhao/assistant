App identity logos

App logos are supplied by Composio through https://logos.composio.dev/api/{toolkit_slug}.
Source URLs and hashes for the bundled originals are recorded in web/app-icons.json.
Embedded scripts are removed; the server also sanitizes SVG before serving it.
Logos and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. They are used
only to identify the services users can connect. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.
